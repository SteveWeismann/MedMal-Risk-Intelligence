    Separation of the two environments

    Parameter P.4 · CHART review comment, 27 Sep

    Risk management and claims evaluation run as two distinct working phases in two separate environments. This is what may cross between them, and what may not.

    CHART has confirmed that an individual may hold both roles. The separation is therefore of the information, not of the people: a dual-role user sees each environment exactly as defined above, nothing protected is written into the claims record, and every switch is recorded on a single audit trail.

    Protections in this environment

    CHART asked that the appropriate protections be built into each environment. These are this environment's own — they change when you switch phase.

    Crossing record

    Parameter P.4 · one trail across both environments

    Because an individual may hold both roles, access control alone does not carry the protection. This is what does: every environment switch, what was open at the time, and what was — or was not — carried across.

    Retention, who may read this trail, and whether it is itself patient safety work product are open items for counsel and the PSO.

    Scope boundaries

    Parameters P.1, P.2, P.3 & P.5

    The limits CHART set in its own objectives. Each is a design constraint in the platform, not a statement of intent.

    How success is measured

    Summary objective S.3 · proof of concept POC.1, POC.2, POC.5

    CHART's test is whether the system goes beyond summaries and timelines to surface what would otherwise need significant manual review.

    Model governance

    Summary objective S.3 · proof of concept POC.1

    What counsel and the PSO will be asked about the system itself, not its output. Recorded per release and carried into the audit trail.

    The platform records an evidentiary grade rather than a numeric confidence score. A percentage attached to a clinical finding invites a question in deposition that nothing in the system can answer; supported / recorded / proposed states what the record contains, which can be shown.

    Phase 2 — carried forward

    Objectives 2.1–2.5 · not in this engagement

    Event reporting and the PSES depend on member participation and a legal structure not yet in place. Deferred deliberately; the Phase 1 data model does not foreclose any of it.

    CHART Risk Intelligence

    Working prototype · MAPS Technologies

    Every number here is invented. This is a design prototype built to agree what the platform should show. No CHART claim information has been processed.

    It is built against CHART’s own Statement of Work objectives — each panel is labelled with the objective it serves, so you can check coverage against your document.

    • Seven tabs across the top, from the daily working view through to reports.
    • Click any row in a table to see the finding, the record it came from, and the page it was cited to.
    • Use the Show control to switch between the full capability and what the first release would actually deliver.
